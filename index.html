PLAYMTG

Play MTG online free: Commander, Draft and ranked duels

PlayMTG Online is a free browser tabletop for Magic: The Gathering fans. Build a deck from every card, then play one-on-one Commander or 60-card games against friends, other players or the computer. The rules engine handles the stack, combat, triggers and mana, so games play like they do on the kitchen table.

Game modes

  • Quick Match: jump in with any deck, with a bot if nobody is around.
  • Commander 1v1 and Standard 60-card duels.
  • Ranked Commander: climb from Bronze to Mythic each season.
  • Draft Night: open three boosters with three other players, build a 40-card deck and play three rounds. Every pick is yours to keep.

Deck builder

Search every card by color, mana value, type and rarity. Import decklists by pasting them or from a Moxfield, Archidekt, MTGGoldfish or Scryfall link, and export for MTG Arena or MTGO.

Free to play

Earn embers by playing, complete daily quests, and unlock sleeves, card backs, playmats and avatars that everyone at your table can see.
